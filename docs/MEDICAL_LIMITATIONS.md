# Medical limitations

EyeRef AI is an **experimental research prototype**. It is not cleared or approved as a medical device
in any jurisdiction. It is a **screening estimate**, **not a prescription**, and it does not replace a
comprehensive eye examination.

## It must never be used to

- prescribe or order glasses or contact lenses;
- rule out eye disease, amblyopia risk or a need for professional care;
- monitor myopia-control treatment clinically;
- triage urgent symptoms.

## Seek urgent professional care for

- sudden loss or dimming of vision, or a curtain or shadow over part of the vision;
- new flashes or showers of floaters;
- eye pain, a red painful eye, trauma or chemical exposure;
- a white, yellow or missing red reflex, or very unequal reflexes, particularly in a child;
- a new squint or double vision.

## Known technical limitations

| Limitation | Effect | Mitigation in the software |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dead zone (eccentricity larger than the pupil) | Emmetropia and low myopia produce no crescent | Interval outputs; calibrated gradient; recommend 1.5 m |
| The eyes focus on the light (no cycloplegia) | An eye that can see the light reads more myopic than it is: hyperopia and mild myopia are hidden, especially in children | Focusing modelled from both eyes up to the amplitude for the age; a number only when the eye is still pinned down, otherwise a range with hyperopia not ruled out; children screening-only |
| Small pupils (bright rooms, age, medication) | Wider dead zone, unusable frames | Dim-room guidance, pupil gate |
| Low pixel count in browser video | Crescent width imprecise | Iris-pixel warning; native app planned |
| Device geometry unknown | Scale errors | Calibration wizard; ±0.5 D added when uncalibrated |
| Fundus pigmentation, iris colour | Reflex brightness varies | Geometry-based estimator; subgroup validation planned |
| Glasses or contact lenses worn | Measures the combined system | Instructions; correction recorded |
| Strabismus, cataract, keratoconus, previous refractive surgery | Unreliable or misleading reflex | Reflex asymmetry flag; exclusion in studies |
| Astigmatism | Needs controlled multi-meridian data | CYL/AXIS gated off by default |

## Output levels

- **Quantitative estimate**: an SE with a 95% interval no wider than ±1.0 D. This is still not a
  prescription.
- **Screening only**: a likely category (myopia, no significant error, hyperopia) with a confidence,
  but no dioptre value; or, when the eyes could be focusing on the light, a range ("no more myopic
  than X") with hyperopia not ruled out, and a category only if the whole range lies past a threshold.
- **Repeat / insufficient confidence**: nothing is estimated.

The printable referral report shows the same gated output on one A4 page for an eye-care
professional. It never prints SPH, CYL or AXIS, even when the research flag has quantified them on
screen, because the paper leaves the app without that context. Simulated assessments carry a
SIMULATED watermark and banner on paper.

The optional AI explanation is generated by a language model (by default a local Gemma model through
Ollama; a hosted service only if configured). It only rephrases the
existing report. A guard removes any dioptre, axis or prescription-like number that is not already in
the report.
